💡 Quick Tips to Reduce Energy Consumption

  • Switch to energy-efficient appliances (5-star rating)
  • Turn off devices when not in use
  • Use power-saving modes on televisions and computers
  • Consider solar panels for renewable energy

Frequently Asked Questions

The average Australian household consumes approximately 16-20 kWh per day, with appliances accounting for about 40% of this usage.

Heating and cooling systems consume the most energy (around 40%), followed by water heating (25%), and entertainment devices like televisions (10%).

Use our interactive energy calculator on the Televisions page. Simply input the wattage, daily usage hours, and electricity price to estimate your consumption.

A kilowatt-hour is a unit of energy equal to 1,000 watt-hours. It's the standard measure used by electricity companies to bill customers.

Appliances with a 5-star energy rating are the most efficient. Look for the Energy Rating Label when purchasing new appliances.

Switching to energy-efficient appliances can save Australian households up to 30% on their electricity bills, which can amount to $400-$500 annually.
