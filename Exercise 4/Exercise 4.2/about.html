Project Overview

This website is part of the COS30045 Data Visualisation unit at Swinburne University of Technology. The project demonstrates the use of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to build an interactive energy consumption tracking website.

Our Mission

We aim to help Australian households understand and reduce their energy consumption through education and practical tools. By visualizing energy usage data, we hope to encourage more sustainable energy habits.
