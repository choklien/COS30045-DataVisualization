🖥️ Does Screen Technology Matter?

LCD vs LCD (LED) vs OLED — which is most efficient?

Energy by Screen Type & Size

Grouped Bar

OLED consumes more energy across ALL size categories.

🔋

OLED: Premium Picture, Premium Energy

OLED TVs consistently consume MORE energy than LCD/LCD (LED) TVs across every size category.

LCD ⚡⚡⚡ 350-610 kWh/yr Most Efficient
LCD (LED) ⚡⚡⚡⚡ 370-620 kWh/yr Efficient
OLED ⚡⚡⚡⚡⚡ 450-700 kWh/yr Energy Hungry
⚠️ Trade-off: OLED offers stunning picture quality — but it comes at a cost for your electricity bill and the environment.

* Based on 4 hours daily use

🛒

Which TV Should You Buy?

✅ Choose LCD (LED) if...

  • • Energy efficiency is your priority
  • • You want lower electricity bills
  • • You watch TV for 4+ hours daily

🎬 Choose OLED if...

  • • Picture quality is your top priority
  • • You're a movie/home theater enthusiast
  • • You're willing to pay for premium experience
💡 The Bottom Line: If energy efficiency is your priority, LCD (LED) is the better choice. If you want the best picture quality and don't mind higher running costs, OLED is for you.
